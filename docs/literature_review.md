# Literature Review

# 1. Introduction

Country risk assessment has been widely studied by international organisations, financial institutions and academic researchers.

Traditional sovereign risk analysis combines macroeconomic fundamentals, institutional quality, governance indicators and political stability.

This project follows an evidence-based approach by integrating publicly available datasets to build an explainable Country Risk Composite Index (CRCI).

---

# 2. World Bank

The World Bank provides one of the largest collections of macroeconomic indicators through its Open Data initiative.

Examples include:

- GDP per Capita
- Inflation
- Population
- Life Expectancy
- GDP Growth
- External Debt
- Trade Indicators

These indicators describe the economic structure of a country.

---

# 3. Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI)

The Worldwide Governance Indicators project measures governance across six dimensions.

## Voice and Accountability

Measures citizen participation and freedom.

## Political Stability

Measures political violence and government stability.

## Government Effectiveness

Measures public service quality and policy implementation.

## Regulatory Quality

Measures government's ability to formulate sound policies.

## Rule of Law

Measures confidence in institutions and legal enforcement.

## Control of Corruption

Measures the extent to which public power is exercised for private gain.

Together, these indicators provide a comprehensive picture of institutional quality.

---

# 4. Sovereign Credit Ratings

Credit rating agencies such as Moody's, Standard & Poor's and Fitch evaluate sovereign creditworthiness.

Their methodologies typically include:

- Economic strength
- Institutional strength
- Fiscal strength
- External vulnerability
- Political risk

Although proprietary, these methodologies consistently combine economic and governance dimensions.

---

# 5. Research Gap

Most existing country risk methodologies are either:

- Proprietary
- Difficult to reproduce
- Dependent on commercial datasets

This project proposes an open-source alternative using publicly available indicators.

---

# 6. Proposed Contribution

The project contributes by providing:

- Automated data collection
- Transparent methodology
- Explainable Machine Learning
- Reproducible Country Risk Composite Index
